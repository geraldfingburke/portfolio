Light betrays the black beneath
The tilted smiles and crooked teeth
Gnashing at the rent decay
A bed for which my heart to lay

Seething silent, soulless screams
Lost among love's shattered dreams
A balm to keep the pain at bay
Though yet go I, my love must stay

Fearing that which does not seek
My heart shall find love's pyrrhic peak
A debt in blood I cannot pay
For words I would not dare to say
